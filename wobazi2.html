Overview · 八字

BaZi is eight characters.

BaZi (八字) literally means “Eight Characters”. It is one of the oldest systems of Chinese metaphysics, with roots in the Tang and Song dynasties. It converts your exact birth date and time into a chart of eight Chinese characters — a cosmic blueprint of who you are.

Those eight characters sit across Four Pillars — Year, Month, Day, and Hour. Each pillar has a Heavenly Stem on top and an Earthly Branch below. Every character carries an element — Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, or Water — and a Yin or Yang polarity. The interplay is what a reading actually reads: personality, strengths, career climate, relationships, and the timing of major turns.

Unlike Western astrology, BaZi is a calendar, not a sky map. It does not watch planets against constellations. It watches the cycles of the Chinese lunisolar calendar — patterns of time that were recorded, refined, and interpreted for more than a thousand years.

Production · 相生

Wood feeds Fire → Fire creates Earth (ash) → Earth bears Metal (ore) → Metal collects Water → Water nourishes Wood. When a “mother” element is present, that part of life tends to grow without being forced.

Control · 相克

Wood parts Earth → Earth dams Water → Water extinguishes Fire → Fire melts Metal → Metal chops Wood. Control is structure, not a curse. Too much of it on a weak element is where struggle shows.

阴阳 · Yīnyáng

The same element, two tempers.

Every element appears twice — Yang (阳) and Yin (阴). Yang is outward, active, expansive. Yin is inward, receptive, subtle. Yang Wood (甲) is an oak: upright, unyielding. Yin Wood (乙) is a vine: flexible, graceful. Both are Wood. They do not live the same life.

That split is why BaZi has ten Day Masters, not five. The balance of Yin and Yang across a chart also shows whether someone leads by assertion or by reception — and where the missing polarity needs to be cultivated.

Twelve animals, sixty years.

合婚 · Héhūn

Compatibility is two full charts.

BaZi has been used for centuries to weigh a pairing — romance, business, friendship. It does not stop at the year animal. A real reading compares both elemental profiles.

  • Elemental balance. Do the two charts cover each other’s excesses and gaps?
  • Day Master interaction. Do the two selves support, control, or clash?
  • Zodiac harmony. Rat and Ox ally; Rat and Horse clash. Tradition still counts.
  • Branch combinations. Six Harmonies and Three Harmonies can bind two charts into one weather.

Questions

Asked before the first chart.

What does BaZi mean?

BaZi (八字) translates to “Eight Characters.” It is the eight Chinese characters — two for each of the Four Pillars — taken from the exact date and time of birth. Together they are treated as a blueprint of temperament, strength, friction, and path.

How is a BaZi chart calculated?

Birth date and time are converted into the Chinese calendar. Each pillar is a Heavenly Stem paired with an Earthly Branch. Stems cycle through ten elemental, Yin–Yang characters. Branches cycle through twelve zodiac animals.

What are the Five Elements?

Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water. Production nourishes the next; Control restrains another. Balance and imbalance in a chart are where personality and fortune are read.

What is a Day Master?

The Heavenly Stem of your Day Pillar. It is the most important single character because it stands for you. Everything else is interpreted in relation to it.

Can BaZi speak to compatibility?

Yes. Two charts are compared for elemental complement, Day Master relation, animal harmony, and branch combinations. Complementary gaps often read as ease; shared excess can read as heat.
